=========================
APP PLANNING & DESIGN
=========================

1. PROJECT NAME & THEME
-------------------------
- What is your app called, and what type of app is it?  RECfood, an app in which users get recommended different foods
- What style or vibe do you want users to experience? Organized, personalized, easy to work with 
- What about your app will require a log-in structure? Being directed to your own food recommendation

2. CORE FUNCTIONALITY
-------------------------
- What are the essential features your app must have? Log in/ register screens, random recommendation 
- What optional features could make your app more interactive or personalized? Specification of the country in which the food is from

3. SECRET OR PERSONALIZED CONTENT

-------------------------
- What content will users see after logging in?  Random food reco
- Will this content change depending on who is using the app (admin, user, special user?) If the user clicks the recommend more button they are taken to a screen with an insult on it 

4. USER EXPERIENCE (UX) DESIGN
-------------------------
- How should your app look and feel? Simple, sleek, professional
- How will users navigate between pages? Buttons that either say log in, create an account, log out, recommend more

5. SECURITY FEATURES
-------------------------
- What password and authentication rules will you enforce?  Username has to include a period at the end 
- How will you protect secret pages from unauthorized access? If the user doesn't click the recommend more button 

6. OPTIONAL CREATIVE ENHANCEMENTS
-------------------------
- What customization or fun features could users enjoy? Food.
- Are there interactive elements, like games, quizzes, or badges, you could include? Fatty badge! :)
